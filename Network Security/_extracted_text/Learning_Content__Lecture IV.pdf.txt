Shannon Principles in Block Cipher DesignClaude Shannon proposed two fundamental ideas for secure cipher design:1. Confusion2. DiffusionConfusion:The relationship between the secret keyand the ciphertextshould be highly complex and hidden. An attacker should not be able to predict how changing the key affects the output.Diffusion:Each plaintext bit influences many ciphertext bits.Small input change ⇒large unpredictable output change.
