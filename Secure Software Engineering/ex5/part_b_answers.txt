1. Five red flags, with evidence:

- Misleading sender domain: "examcell@bits-wilp-results.net" does not use an
  official BITS Pilani domain.
- Urgency: "verify in 24 hours".
- Threat of a consequence: "your Mid-Semester result has been withheld" and
  "To avoid cancelation of your registration".
- Deceptive link: "https://elearn.bits-pilani.ac.in.verify-login.co/results"
  contains trusted-looking words but does not end in bits-pilani.ac.in.
- Unusual financial request: "confirm your bank card for a refund of Rs 1,250."

2. The effective domain is verify-login.co. Reading the hostname from the
   right, the registrable domain is verify-login.co; elearn.bits-pilani.ac.in
   is only a subdomain controlled by that domain.

3. I would open the official BITS/WILP website or contact the exam cell using
   a known phone number or address from an official source, not the email.
   I would also check the result through the normal eLearn portal by typing
   its known address manually and report the message to the institution.
